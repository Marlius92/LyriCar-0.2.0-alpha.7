LyriCar 0.1.5-alpha.6 — Sub-pixel transition report
====================================================

Root cause
----------
0.1.4-alpha.5 already kept the Tk event loop close to 60 Hz, but font size was
quantized in two-point steps. With a 1.2-second transition, the active line had
only 10 effective sizes and could remain unchanged for 11 consecutive frames.
The perceived stutter was therefore geometric quantization, not primarily a
playback-clock or frame-pacing failure.

Implementation
--------------
- high-resolution Pillow glyph masks;
- quarter-pixel scale units;
- asynchronous preparation of the next transition;
- one reusable RGB ImageTk surface instead of per-line Tk font resizing;
- direct high-resolution-to-display mask resize;
- absolute 60 Hz deadline retained;
- transition curve: 20% linear + 80% smoothstep;
- matching softened curve in SwiftUI.

Scale continuity at 1.2 seconds / 73 samples
---------------------------------------------
- old two-point renderer: 10 effective sizes;
- new sub-pixel renderer: 61 effective sizes;
- longest consecutive hold: 11 frames -> 3 frames.

Synthetic Tk benchmark
----------------------
Environment: Linux/Xvfb, Tk 8.6, logical Renault profile 600 x 800, one cold
preheater plus a full 1.2-second transition.

- rendered frames: 197;
- mean frame interval: 16.85 ms;
- 95th percentile frame interval: 17.45 ms;
- mean draw time: 1.71 ms;
- 95th percentile draw time: 5.71 ms;
- maximum interval: 46.93 ms during cold cache preparation.

The benchmark is a development comparison, not a guarantee for every Windows
machine. The target-PC Spotify test remains authoritative.
